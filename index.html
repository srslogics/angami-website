Brand Direction

A modern agro-processing company with a premium regional identity.

What Angami should communicate

The company should not feel like only a juice brand or only a factory. Its strength is the blend of sourcing, processing, wellness positioning, and industrial credibility.

Primary audiences

The website needs to speak to distributors, bulk buyers, export partners, and retail-facing stakeholders at the same time without making the business feel confusing.

Best website posture

Present Angami Foods as a premium Northeast India-based natural extracts and food processing company with strong product storytelling and a clear route for business enquiries.

Capabilities

SCFE extraction and clean processing should be one of the website’s biggest trust builders.

Supercritical Fluid Extraction

The brochure’s strongest differentiator is the SCFE plant. This deserves a dedicated spotlight on the homepage and its own detail page later.

  • Pure CO₂-based extraction process
  • No harmful solvent residues
  • Suitable for oils and oleoresins from ginger, herbs, botanicals, fruits, and roots
  • Eco-conscious, science-led positioning

Fruit Processing

Orange juice, pineapple juice, and canned pineapple slices create a more consumer-ready and commercial product dimension.

Spice & Wellness

Turmeric, ginger, and black pepper products extend the brand into daily wellness and pantry use.

Bulk Potential

The website should clearly invite enquiries for ingredients, institutional packaging, and distributor conversations.

Origin Story

Northeast India is not a side note. It is part of the brand value.

The brochure repeatedly points toward the agricultural advantage of the region. That gives the website a powerful story around freshness, chemical-light cultivation, and a more distinctive source identity than generic spice brands.

The website should talk about proximity to farms, regional fruit abundance, cleaner cultivation context, and how local sourcing connects to product quality.

“From the fruit belts and farms of Northeast India to modern processing and premium packaged products.”

Quality Story

The brochure hints at trust. The website should make that trust explicit.

What should be highlighted

  • Solvent-free extraction benefits
  • Hygienic processing and sealing
  • Food-grade packaging
  • Shelf-life and freshness protection

What is still needed from the client

  • Certifications and licenses
  • Factory photographs
  • Testing and compliance details
  • Export or bulk supply information
